# Official Alignment Notes

The prototype terminology follows the supplied Land Stack challenge statement. For the ULPIN/land-governance framing, the Department of Land Resources describes ULPIN as a parcel identifier based on georeferenced cadastral information and notes alignment with ECCMA and OGC standards. The Department also describes DILRMP goals around integrated land information, transparency, sharing of information, reduction of disputes and fraudulent transactions, and reducing physical visits.

For a presentation, cite the current official Department of Land Resources pages rather than presenting synthetic AcreNex data as government data.

Suggested official references:
- Department of Land Resources — ULPIN / Bhu-Aadhaar
- Department of Land Resources — DILRMP
- Department of Land Resources — Land Stack launch, 31 December 2025
- Department of Land Resources — NAKSHA portal / documentation

Important: AcreNex is a prototype concept. It is not an official Government of India application and its demo database is synthetic.
